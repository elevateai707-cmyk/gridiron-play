Gridiron Legends: privacy policy

Developer: Build With DB, Alberta, Canada
Effective date: 2026-11-12

In short: the game collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not connect to the internet. Your game progress stays on your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Gridiron Legends does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. We never receive any data from your device.

No network access

The Android app does not ask for the internet permission. It cannot send or receive data over a network. All of the game's pictures, sounds, music and fonts are inside the app.

No accounts, ads or analytics

  • There is no sign-in and no account.
  • There are no ads.
  • There are no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools inside the game.
  • We do not use your advertising ID, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

What is saved on your device

The game saves your progress (for example your season, results and standings) and your settings (for example volume and difficulty). This data is saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage.

It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in Android settings. The app turns off Android's cloud backup, so this data is not copied to a Google account by the app.

Permissions

The app uses one permission: vibration, so that your phone can give small buzzes during play. It does not use the permission for anything else.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no online play, no purchases and no links that lead out of the app.

Google Play

You install the game through Google Play. Google may collect its own information about installs and, if you have agreed to share it, crash and performance statistics for apps on your phone. That is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one. We do not see who you are from those statistics.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date before the change is released.
